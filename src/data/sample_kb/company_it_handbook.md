# NovaRetail Company IT Handbook

## VPN Access

Employees working remotely must connect to the company VPN before accessing any internal resources.

**Steps to connect (Windows):**
1. Open the GlobalProtect VPN client from the system tray.
2. Enter `vpn.novaretail.internal` as the portal address.
3. Sign in with your NovaRetail SSO credentials.
4. Wait for the "Connected" status before accessing internal tools.

**macOS / Linux:** Follow the same steps using the GlobalProtect app downloaded from the IT Portal.

If you encounter connection issues, contact the service desk at `helpdesk@novaretail.com`.

## Password Policy

- Minimum 14 characters.
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, a digit, and a special character.
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- You cannot reuse the last 10 passwords.
- After 5 failed attempts your account is locked; contact IT to unlock.

**Never share your password with anyone, including IT support personnel.**

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for all employees.

- Use the Microsoft Authenticator app.
- If you lose access to your MFA device, call the IT helpdesk directly — do **not** share MFA codes with support staff via email or chat.
- IT support will **never** ask you for your MFA code.

---

## Software Installation Policy

Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices.

Approved software list is maintained in the IT Portal at `portal.novaretail.internal/approved-software`.

To request new software, submit a ticket at `helpdesk@novaretail.com` with a business justification.

Installing unapproved software is a policy violation and may result in disciplinary action.

## Laptop Troubleshooting

**Laptop won't boot:**
1. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force shutdown.
2. Wait 30 seconds, then restart.
3. If the issue persists, boot into Safe Mode (hold Shift on Windows login screen).
4. Contact IT if Safe Mode also fails.

**Slow performance:**
- Check Task Manager / Activity Monitor for high CPU/memory processes.
- Restart the device.
- Ensure Windows/macOS updates are applied.

## Acceptable Use

Company devices and network are for business use.
Personal use is permitted in moderation but must not:
- Compromise network security.
- Violate any laws.
- Interfere with business operations.
